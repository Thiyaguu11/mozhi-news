Vijay's first week: electricity, women's safety, and a floor test passed

Six days in, the new CM signs two flagship orders and clears the confidence vote. What actually happened — without the spin.

Six days into his tenure, Tamil Nadu's new Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has moved quickly on two of his government's flagship promises. On the day of his swearing-in, he signed orders for 200 units of free electricity for domestic consumers and established a dedicated women's safety unit — two commitments central to the TVK manifesto.

The government also cleared a confidence vote in the Assembly, crossing the 118-seat majority mark with the support of coalition partners. In his first address as CM, Vijay struck a deliberately measured tone.

"I am not some divine messenger. I will never deceive you with false promises. I will only promise what is possible," he said at the secretariat.

The Calm Take

What to watch: The real test for this government is not the first week — it's the first budget and the welfare scheme rollouts. Whether the coalition arithmetic holds under fiscal pressure will define Vijay's tenure far more than the swearing-in spectacle.
