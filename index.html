About Mozhi

Built for people who want facts, not fire.

Tamil news has a noise problem. Every channel, every feed, every WhatsApp forward is engineered to provoke. Mozhi exists to be different.

We cover Tamil Nadu politics, governance, and global Tamil affairs in plain Tamil and English — factual, bilingual, and calm. No screaming anchors. No partisan framing. No engagement-bait headlines.

We started with Vijay becoming CM in May 2026 — a genuinely historic moment in Tamil Nadu — and we'll keep covering whatever matters next: policy decisions, diaspora stories, cultural shifts, and the slow work of governance.
